# Code of Conduct

Be direct, be kind, assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.

Not welcome here: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption. The maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or ban participants who behave that way, at their discretion.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any chat the project runs.
